# Alyamama & Alexandria: Typography Usage Report

Oct 18, 2026

## Overview

The website now uses exactly two typefaces: Alyamama for editorial content (headlines, quotes, body text, excerpts) and Alexandria for the interface (navigation, tags, buttons, metadata). Across the 24 visible website artboards, Alexandria appears in 565 text runs and Alyamama in 282.

The file has 10 local text styles: 6 `Primary/…` styles, all Alyamama, and 4 `Secondary/…` styles, all Alexandria. Where text uses a different weight from its style's default, it is listed under that style.

| Font | Text runs | Weights used | Sizes used (px) | Text styles (used) | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Alyamama | 282 | Light, Regular, Medium | 14–36 | 6 (6) | Editorial content |
| Alexandria | 565 | Light, Regular, Medium | 12–18 | 5 (4) | Interface and metadata |

## Alyamama: editorial content

Alyamama sets everything a reader reads as journalism, all of it through six text styles. Medium carries every title, headline and quote in two sizes, Regular carries long-form reading, and Light carries the excerpts under headlines.

## Desktop vs mobile

On mobile, Alyamama collapses every title, headline and quote into one style, Primary/Heading 2 (Medium 22), and keeps the weight. Alexandria shrinks a few interface elements and keeps tags, navigation and dates unchanged.

| Role | Font | Desktop | Mobile |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Page title | Alyamama | Medium 36 | Medium 22 |
| Lead headline (hero, Article title) | Alyamama | Medium 36 | Medium 22 |
| Statement quote | Alyamama | Medium 36 | Medium 22 |
| Pull quote in Article | Alyamama | Medium 36 | Medium 22 |
| Call-to-action text | Alyamama | Medium 36 | Medium 22 |
| Card headline | Alyamama | Medium 22 | Medium 22 |
| In-article subheading | Alyamama | Medium 22 | Regular 20 |
| Long-form body | Alyamama | Regular 20 | Regular 16 (Article, About); Regular 20 (Statement, team bios) |
| Excerpt | Alyamama | Light 16 / Light 14 | Light 14 |
| Section header | Alexandria | Regular 18 | Regular 14 |
| "Submit a claim" button | Alexandria | Regular 18 | Regular 12 |
| Sources accordion title | Alexandria | Regular 18 | Regular 12 |
| Footer links | Alexandria | Regular 14 | Light 12 |
| Team role title | Alexandria | Regular 18 | Regular 18 |
| Tags, navigation, dates | Alexandria | Unchanged | Unchanged |

Primary/Heading 2 is also the desktop card-headline and subheading style: 46 of its 123 runs are on desktop artboards.
